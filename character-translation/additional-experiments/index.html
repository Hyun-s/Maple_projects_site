Can 30 learned tokens compose unseen Maple outfits?

This end-to-end milestone rendered every combination of ten hats, ten tops, and ten bottoms, trained joint SDXL textual inversion, and evaluated 100 combinations excluded from training.

Scientific verdict Negative result, published without selection

The pipeline completed, but the learned model scored below a shuffled renderer control on CLIP and LPIPS. Reliable cosmetic binding was not established.

03 / Held-out results

The controls pass; item-token composition does not

The first 100 test combinations used 30 diffusion steps and CFG 7.0. Oracle verifies exact matching. Shuffled assigns another real renderer image as a negative control.

Model under test

Joint TI

Joint textual inversion. All 30 hat, top, and bottom identifier tokens were learned together while the rest of SDXL stayed frozen. This is the only row produced by the trained model.

Perfect-match check

Oracle

Not a model output. The exact ground-truth image is copied and compared with itself. Its perfect scores confirm that image loading, pairing, and metric code recognize an exact match.

Wrong-outfit control

Shuffled

Not a model output. Each target is paired with a different real renderer image using a fixed, no-self-match shuffle. It exposes scores caused by the shared white background, body, pose, and sprite style rather than the requested outfit.

How to read the comparison: a convincing item-binding result should move Joint TI toward Oracle and outperform Shuffled on identity-sensitive evidence. Here, Joint TI falls below Shuffled on CLIP and LPIPS, so the run does not establish reliable cosmetic binding.

04 / Verdict

Pipeline completion is not evidence of compositional identity

The learned outputs often resemble generic chibi characters, but their requested hat, top, and bottom identities are weaker than another real outfit selected at random. Some outputs also introduce frame artifacts.

  1. 01

    Separate data scale from repeated-exposure compute with fixed-step and fixed-epoch controls.

  2. 02

    Render category-only paired targets and add item retrieval accuracy instead of relying on white-background pixels.

  3. 03

    Compare joint TI, independent TI, and LoRA plus learned identifiers under the exact same split and seeds.

  4. 04

    Test a domain-LoRA-first condition to isolate sprite-domain mismatch from token-binding failure.

Metric scope: CLIP and LPIPS compare the whole normalized image. No per-item mask was used; category-conditioned summaries are therefore not localized garment scores.
