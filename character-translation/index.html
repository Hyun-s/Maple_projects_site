Maple Custom: Compositional Cosmetic-Item Inversion & Character Translation

Hyunsoo Han1
1Applied Machine Learning Research
Technical Report Code Repository 3k–90k Checkpoint Audit Inversion Study Projects Hub

Can text-to-image diffusion models learn the visual identity of individual 2D pixel-art cosmetic items and recombine them arbitrarily into novel high-quality character illustrations?
LoRA rank-16 achieves distributional fidelity on paired character translation, whereas naive textual inversion fails due to canvas occupancy imbalance and asymmetric text-encoder drift.

Abstract

We study compositional item inversion and character translation from a controlled 10 hats × 10 tops × 10 bottoms renderer dataset and a paired character-translation benchmark. Each of the 1,000 combinations is represented by a 2D MapleStory avatar sprite and a caption containing three item-specific tokens. We compare SDXL and FLUX.1-dev textual inversion, anime-domain prompt interventions, and a 3,000 to 90,000-step optimization sweep. We also benchmark conventional zero-shot, LoRA, and learned-token adaptation on 98 held-out paired illustration pairs.

Our findings report a conclusive scientific result: while conventional SDXL LoRA significantly improves distributional fidelity over zero-shot inference (FID: 217.70 vs 265.68), naive textual inversion across both SDXL and FLUX fails to recover reliable item identity. Extending FLUX training to 90,000 steps continuously lowers flow-matching velocity loss without yielding compositional binding. Empirical failure analysis identifies that raw source sprites occupy only 2.73% of the canvas area, causing background noise gradients to dominate, and CLIP-L token embeddings drift by 104.22%. We introduce a foreground normalization pipeline (75% occupancy) paired with domain-specific LoRA adaptation to resolve this representational mismatch.

Methodology

Adaptation Objectives & Parameterization
SDXL Denoising MSE
\[ \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SDXL}} = \mathbb{E}_{x,\epsilon,t}\left[\lVert \epsilon - \epsilon_{\theta}(z_t,t,c) \rVert_2^2\right] \]
&
FLUX Velocity Prediction & LoRA
\[ \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{FLUX}} = \mathbb{E}\left[\lVert v_{\theta}(x_t,t,c) - (x_1 - x_0) \rVert_2^2\right], \quad \Delta W = \frac{\alpha}{r}BA \]
&circledone; Smart Foreground Normalization
Automated bounding-box detection rescales raw sprites to 75% canvas occupancy using nearest-neighbor interpolation, eliminating background dominance.
\[ \text{Occupancy} = \frac{\text{Area}(\text{bbox})}{\text{Area}(\text{canvas})} \to 0.75 \]
&circledtwo; Shared Domain LoRA
Pre-trains a rank-16 transformer LoRA on normalized sprites to internalize the pixel domain prior to individual cosmetic token binding.
\[ r=16, \ \alpha=16, \ \text{lr}=10^{-4} \]
&circledthree; Lineart Contour Conditioning
Extracts pixel-exact silhouettes and lineart condition maps to enforce contour preservation during sprite-to-illustration translation.
\[ x_{\mathrm{cond}} = \text{Sobel}(\text{sprite}) \otimes M_{\mathrm{fg}} \]
