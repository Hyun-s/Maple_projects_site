Research Statement & Evaluation Philosophy

Modern applied machine learning systems in complex domains require transparent engineering boundaries. Rather than publishing ungrounded demonstrations or cherry-picked generative outputs, this portfolio documents end-to-end architectures, verified benchmark metrics, negative optimization results, and strict boundary disclosures. All reported quantitative and qualitative artifacts are statically auditable.

03 / Working Principles

Evidence Before Claims

The portfolio adheres to four engineering standards designed to keep technical evaluation rigorous and auditable.

PRINCIPLE 01

State the Evidence

Every performance claim and metric is anchored to a specific snapshot date, dataset boundary, and clear validation status rather than informal benchmarks.

PRINCIPLE 02

Show the Boundary

Public architectures, configs, and methodology remain completely decoupled from private operational secrets, tokens, endpoints, and proprietary corpora.

PRINCIPLE 03

Keep Failures Visible

Observed optimization failures (such as 90k FLUX drift or RAG semantic collisions) are preserved with diagnoses, root causes, and regression checks.

PRINCIPLE 04

Prefer Static Records

Core technical records, visual audits, and evaluation metrics remain fast, deterministic, and permanently inspectable without requiring live backend dependencies.
